Questions

Good to know

What kind of product photo do I need?

Any clear photo of the product works: on a hanger, laid flat, or on a table. Better light gives better results, but you do not need a studio.

Will the AI images look like my actual product?

Yes. Hookd keeps your fabric, color, print and fit. You review every image before anything is posted.

Do I have to post through Hookd for the agent to reply?

No. Once your Instagram account is connected, the agent answers comments on every post, whether you published it with Hookd, the Instagram app or another tool.

How does the agent know what to say?

It reads each post into memory along with your prices, sizes and store policies. You set the rules for what it answers and what it sends to you.

Can I check replies before they go out?

Yes. Start in review mode and approve each reply. Switch to autopilot when you trust it.

Which platforms do you support?

Instagram today. YouTube is coming soon.
