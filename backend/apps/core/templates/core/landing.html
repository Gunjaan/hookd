Safe by design

The guardrails are code, not a prompt.

When a tool says no, Claude gets the reason back and fixes its next step.

  • No made-up prices

    A number guard rejects any figure that is not in your brain or the chat. Hooky has to look it up or ask you.

  • Paced like a person

    One message at a time, 45 to 75 seconds apart. At most 20 an hour and 150 a day.

  • Follows Meta's DM rules

    DMs only inside the 24 hour window, or one private reply to a comment within 7 days.

  • Never runs in circles

    8 steps a turn and 40 a run. After that the buyer is handed to you.

  • Asks you sparingly

    3 questions a run at most. An unanswered one expires after 24 hours.

  • You stay in control

    The agent starts off. Approval mode, an off switch, and a Try it box that sends nothing.

Questions

Good to know

How does the agent know what to say?

It answers from your brain: your prices, sizes, delivery and store policies, plus what it reads from each post. When your brain has the answer it replies on its own, when it does not it asks you first.

What happens when it does not know the answer?

It asks you instead of guessing. Your reply goes to the buyer and is saved to the brain, so the next buyer with the same question gets an instant answer.

Can it make up a price?

No. A number guard in our code checks every reply and DM. Any price or figure that is not in your brain or the conversation is rejected, so Hooky has to look it up or ask you.

Will Instagram flag my account?

Hookd replies only on your own posts, spaces replies out, caps them per hour and per day, and never sends the same text over and over.

Can I check replies before they go out?

Yes. Switch on approval mode and every reply and DM waits in your inbox until you send it. Switch it off when you trust Hooky to reply on its own.

Do I have to post through Hookd for the agent to reply?

No. Once your Instagram account is connected, the agent answers comments on every post, whether you published it with Hookd, the Instagram app or another tool.

Can Hookd make my product photos too?

Yes, as a bonus. Upload one product photo and the studio creates on model shots, a caption and hashtags. You review everything before it is posted.

Which platforms do you support?

Instagram today. YouTube is coming soon.
